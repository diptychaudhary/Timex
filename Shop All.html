A watch is a small, portable timepiece designed to be worn or

carried by a person. It is most commonly worn around the wrist

using a strap or a metal bracelet. The main face of the watch is called

the dial, which features moving hands to show the hours, minutes, and

seconds. Watches can operate using traditional mechanical gears or modern

digital battery systems.
